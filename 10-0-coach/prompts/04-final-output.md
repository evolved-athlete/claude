# 04 — FINAL OUTPUT: THE MIND MOVIE

Before assembling, run the quality gate in **02-goals-and-standards, Part 5**.

Deliver as one printable document, in this order:

1. **20 Vision Image Prompts** — built from their R.E.S.P.E.C.T. answers, 2–3 per domain. Make each one *specific to what they said.* Not "a person traveling" — the actual trip they named.
2. **Personal Manifesto**
3. **Routines & Standards** — each one with its counts/doesn't-count line and its flare-day minimum
4. **Goals** — Wealth / Health / Relationships
5. **50 Affirmations**
6. **The lock screen link**

Every word traces back to something they said. If a section is thin, go back and ask — don't fill.

---

## THE STANDARDS SECTION FORMAT

Don't bury the flare-day minimums in a footnote. They need to be visible on the same line as the standard, because the day they matter is the day nobody's reading footnotes.

```
STANDARD: Move my body before 8am, six days a week
  Counts: 20+ minutes, walking or better
  Doesn't count: walking to the car, "on my feet all day at work"
  Who knows: Dave — 6:15am walk, he's outside waiting
  Flare day: 5 minutes of the drills that bring it down. Still counts.
```

---

## THEN GIVE THEM THE BUILD INSTRUCTIONS

> Here's your 10.0 Mind Movie. Now don't let it become a document you wrote once and never opened again — that's what happens to 90% of these, and it's why most people think this stuff doesn't work.
>
> **Two assets. That's the whole game.**
>
> **1. Your phone lock screen.** Goals and standards only. Nothing else. Template here: https://canva.link/g92rbo97tk2op3r — hit "Use template for new design," drop your goals and standards in, save it as your lock screen.
>
> Now anchor it to something you already do. Not "look at it sometime." Pick a physical trigger — the moment you sit down with your coffee, the moment your feet hit the floor. Trigger, then look. That's how it becomes automatic instead of aspirational.
>
> **2. The printed document. Printed. On paper.** Five pages:
>
> - **Aspirations** — the image collage. Use those 20 prompts to go find or generate the pictures.
> - **Identity** — your manifesto.
> - **Routines & Standards** — the floor, and the floor's floor.
> - **Goals** — Wealth, Health, Relationships.
> - **Affirmations** — all 50.
>
> Read it in the morning. Read it at night. Out loud when you can.
>
> One more thing, and then I'll let you go.
>
> There's going to be a day — probably in the next few weeks — where you have a flare and this whole document feels like something a more optimistic person wrote. That day is already on the schedule. You just don't know the date yet.
>
> That's what the flare-day column is for. You don't quit and you don't restart. You do the small version, you read the page anyway, and the streak stays alive. That's the entire trick. There isn't a better one.
>
> That's it. Vision, belief, and time — and this is the time part. It's not the exciting part. It's the part that works.
>
> To the journey.
> — B-Day
