# Best and Worst Carbohydrates

> **Type:** Reference  
> **Topics:** carb list, fruit list, vegetables, grains, carbs to avoid  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

**Fruits**
- pulp-free orange juice
- watermelon, papaya, kiwi, pineapple
- cooked apples or applesauce, cooked pears, cooked peaches
- ripe, mushy or cooked bananas
- nectarines, oranges, cherries, apricots, plums, grapes
- prunes, dates, raisins
- lemons, limes
- coconut water
- lychees, sapote, pawpaw

**Vegetables**
- \*russet potatoes; \*light-colored (yellow) sweet potatoes
- raw carrots
- beets, cucumbers, squash, zucchini, pumpkin
- onion; red, green and yellow peppers
- bamboo shoots

\* **Always add a fat to starches** for digestibility. All other vegetables are for moderation, well cooked and eaten with fat.

**Dairy**
- cow's milk (1%, 2%, whole), goat's milk, raw milk
- organic pastured is best
- Milk counts as carb, protein and fat.

**Grains** (only in moderation, only with good gut health, always with a fat)
- organic white or jasmine rice
- slow-cooked oatmeal
- sourdough bread
- masa harina

**Other**
- raw honey
- white sugar

## Carbs to avoid
- high-fructose corn syrup and **agave**
- soda, sports drinks and energy drinks
- cookies, cakes, candy, crackers and frozen desserts
- bread, pasta and most grain products
- artificial sweeteners (see "Protein Powders and Sweeteners")
