# Common Client Questions (Book FAQ)

> **Type:** Reference  
> **Topics:** FAQ, weight gain, how long, thirst, eating out  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

- **"Will I gain weight?"**
  - Possibly at first, especially if coming from low-carb, low-calorie or over-exercise.
  - Slow changes minimize the gain.
  - Warmth, sleep and energy improve before weight drops.
- **"How long does it take?"** Three months to several years. It is ongoing, because new stressors keep appearing.
- **"How many changes at once?"** One or two. Build the habit, then add the next. Too many at once leads to overwhelm and quitting.
- **"I'm always thirsty and peeing."** Probably overhydrated and under-mineralized. Shift fluids to milk, OJ and broth, add salt, and reduce plain water.
- **"I feel hot when I eat more — is that normal?"** Yes. Food used as energy makes heat, and so does salt. It's a sign the metabolism is rising.
- **"How much bone broth?"** 1–2 cups/day, and 1 cup with any muscle meat. With very poor digestion, up to 50% of protein can come from broth and gelatin.
- **"My temperature and pulse dropped after my workout."** The session was too long, too hard or too intense. Cut duration or intensity, or add rest.
- **"Should I become a fat burner?"** No. Use glucose for activity. Fat is burned at rest and during sleep, and more muscle helps.
- **"Can I eat out?"** Yes, 1–2×/week. Ask for butter or olive oil, grass-fed meat and organic produce.
- **"Is juice too much sugar?"** Pair it with protein and fat unless the metabolism is already high. Add it slowly. Use pulp-free.
- **"Headaches?"** Usually blood sugar. Eat small, balanced meals more often and include enough good sugar.
- **"Potatoes every meal?"** Fine if healthy. For gut healing or fat loss, once a day at breakfast or lunch, rinsed, well cooked and with fat.
- **"Alcohol?"** Avoid while healing. Otherwise drink with food and/or juice.
- **"Where do I start?"** Slow, constant changes. Log daily. Work with a practitioner and ask your doctor questions.
