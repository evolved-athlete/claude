# Where the Book Contradicts Itself

> **Type:** Reference  
> **Topics:** inconsistencies, which answer to give  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

| Topic | Chapter text | Appendix / elsewhere | Suggested coach answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grains | Avoid all grains while healing | Appendix A lists rice, oatmeal, sourdough and masa as optimal "in moderation, with good gut health" | Avoid while healing. Reintroduce the Appendix A grains once digestion is good. |
| Shellfish | 1–2×/week | 1–3×/week | 1–3×/week |
| Muscle meat | 2–3×/week if healthy | "1×/week or less if inflamed" | 2–3×/week healthy; ≤1×/week or none if inflamed or hypometabolic |
| Body temperature | 97.8–98.6 °F | FAQ says 98–99 °F | ≥ 97.8 °F on waking, ~98.6 °F midday |
| Nuts | 1–2×/week (Ch. 11) | 1–2×/month (Appendix B) | 1–2×/month while healing; up to 1–2×/week with good gut health |
| Broth simmer time | 12–72 h (Ch. 8) | "at least 24 h" for beef (recipe) | Beef 24–72 h; chicken or fish 8–12 h |
| Gelatin in soup recipe | — | "12 T." | Treat as a typo |
