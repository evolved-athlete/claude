# How Much Protein

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** protein needs, grams per day, protein table, too much protein  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Super Proteins · Inflammatory Amino Acids

- Protein is essential for structure, enzymes, hormones, transport proteins, antibodies and fluid balance.
- It is **a poor daily energy source**. Burning it produces ammonia, urea and lactic acid.
- **Too much protein** slows metabolism, raises stress hormones, disrupts sleep and breaks down muscle. **Too little** is equally stressful.
- Transport proteins (albumin, SHBG, thyroid-binding proteins) fall with **fasting, starvation and stress**, so hormones are delivered less well.

## How much
- RDA minimum: 0.8 g/kg. Active people may need 2–3 times that.
- **Ray Peat:** at least **80 g/day**; **130–150 g/day** for an active, high-metabolism person.
- **Author:** 100–120 g/day at 120 lb and active, about 25% of calories.
- **Hypometabolic clients** have low HCl and digest protein poorly. Prioritize **easy-to-digest** protein.
- **Plant-based eaters** may need more total protein, because anti-nutrients reduce digestibility.

**Protein guidelines for a healthy person (g/day)**

| Body weight | Sedentary | Active |
|---|---|---|
| 100–125 lb | 60–90 | 90–125 |
| 126–150 lb | 70–100 | 100–150 |
| 151–175 lb | 80–110 | 110–175 |
| 176–200 lb | 100–120 | 120–200 |
| >200 lb | 120+ | 180+ |

**Signs protein is about right:**
- warm body (~98.6 °F) and pulse 75–90
- good digestion and sleep
- good muscle tone, libido and mood
- blood pressure about 120/80
- healthy hair, skin, nails, bones and teeth

If these are off, check protein, but also check the carb and fat balance.
