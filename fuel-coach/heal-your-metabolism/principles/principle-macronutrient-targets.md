# Macronutrient Targets

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** macros, carb percentage, protein percentage, fat percentage  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** The Pairing Rule: Carb + Protein + Fat · How Much Protein

| Macro | Range | Starting point | Adjust |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrate | 40–70% | ~50% | Start lower if inactive or more metabolically damaged. Go higher if athletic, young or high-metabolism. Raise as healing progresses. The author eats 55–60%, more when training. |
| Protein | 20–30% | ~25% | Slows the blood-sugar rise from carbs and extends satiety. Too much without carbs and fat triggers a stress response. |
| Fat | 20–30% | — | Damaged metabolisms need more fat, especially **women with hormonal issues** (lost period, infertility) or after long low-fat dieting. Lower fat and raise carbs as healing progresses. |
