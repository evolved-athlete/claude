# Exercise and the Thyroid

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** exercise, HIIT, CrossFit, endurance, overtraining, thyroid suppression  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Exercise While Healing · Weight Training Return Protocol · Workout Fueling

**Core claim:** exercise is a stressor. Strenuous cardio *and* heavy or high-intensity training are associated with lower thyroid function.
- HIIT, CrossFit and similar training don't raise resting metabolic rate. The extra burn comes from work plus adrenaline and cortisol.
- A healthy body recovers fast, especially with sugar and minerals afterward. A damaged one stays suppressed.

**When healthy**
- **No more than 2–3 intense sessions/week**, with full recovery.
- Training intensely 5–7 days/week leads to over-training and a depressed thyroid.
- **Moderate weight training** builds muscle, which means more fat burned at rest. The author's return: **≤45 minutes, 4–5×/week**.
