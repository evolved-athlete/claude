# The Pairing Rule: Carb + Protein + Fat

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** food combining, never eat carbs alone, protein alone, milk  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Blood-Sugar Balancing Rules · Macronutrient Targets

- **Never eat carbohydrate alone, and never eat protein alone.** Every meal and snack should have carb + protein + fat.
- **Carb alone** (fruit, juice, sugar, even root vegetables) gives a fast spike and an insulin surge.
- **Protein alone** raises insulin, because insulin carries amino acids into cells. Without carbs, blood sugar drops, then adrenaline and cortisol rise and muscle is broken down. The usual sign is feeling light-headed after eating eggs or meat on their own.
- **Exception: milk** already contains carb, protein and fat in balance (about 8 g of each per cup of whole milk).
- Example pairings:
  - eggs + orange juice
  - fruit + cheese
  - milk + fruit
  - potatoes + butter + fish
  - coffee + cream + sugar
