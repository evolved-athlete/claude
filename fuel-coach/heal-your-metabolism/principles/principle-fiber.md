# The Fiber Argument

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** fiber, constipation, bloating, gut healing  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

- Gut damage kills beneficial bacteria, so stools harden. Adding fiber bulks the stool but doesn't heal the gut, and may irritate an inflamed lining.
- **Gut-healing swap:**
  - fewer raw salads and grains
  - more bone broth and gelatin
  - cook all fruit and vegetables in butter or coconut oil
  - add eggs and dairy
- In a case example, bloating resolved in about 2 weeks and stools normalized by about 6 months.
