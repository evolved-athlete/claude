# Eating More: Calories and What to Expect

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** calories, eat more work out less, weight gain while healing, timeline  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Large calorie increases for medically complex clients, or anyone with an eating-disorder history, need provider involvement.

- The book's approach is "eat more, work out less." It is "not a diet."
- **Increase food slowly.** Jumping calories quickly causes weight gain.
- Author example (120 lb woman):
  - **1,750–1,900 kcal** on rest days
  - **2,000–2,200 kcal** on training days
  - more when training for an event
- Case example: a client went from 1,200–1,500 kcal to about 2,500 kcal over 3 months. She gained some weight while healing, then settled at 1,800–2,000 kcal and was leaner by 6 months.
- Front-load calories. In a case example, the extra 500 kcal/day was **all added before dinner**: bigger breakfast and lunch, smaller dinner. "Breakfast and lunch set the tone" for the day's metabolic rate.

## Expected course
1. The body **warms up** and symptoms improve first: energy, sleep, digestion, mood.
2. **Weight loss usually comes last.** Some people gain weight at first, especially coming off low-carb, low-calorie or over-exercise.
3. The timeline runs from **3 months to several years**. It is an ongoing practice, not a destination.
