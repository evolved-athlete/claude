# Metabolism, Thyroid and Cellular Respiration

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** metabolism, thyroid, ATP, heat, low thyroid  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Thyroid vs. Stress Hormones · Effects of a Sluggish Thyroid

- A well-working metabolism takes glucose into the cell and burns it in the mitochondria to make ATP, **heat, CO₂ and water**.
- More glucose burned efficiently means more heat and energy, which means a higher metabolic rate.
- The book treats **"low thyroid" and "low metabolic rate" as the same thing**.
- The thyroid is "the conductor of every metabolic process."
- In the 1950s Broda Barnes estimated that up to 40% of Americans had low thyroid function. The author believes the figure is higher now, even when labs look normal.
