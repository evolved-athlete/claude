# Why Milk: The Book's "Perfect Food"

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** milk, dairy, calcium, phosphorus, PTH, bone density  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Choosing Milk, Cheese and Yogurt · Lactose Reintroduction Protocol

- Whole milk is about 87% water, 4.6% lactose, 3.4% protein and 4.2% fat.
- Its fat is about 70% saturated and **<2.5% PUFA**.
- **About 8 g each of carb, protein and fat per cup**, which makes it a natural blood-sugar regulator.
- Rich in calcium, magnesium, potassium, selenium, and vitamins A, B2, B12, D and K.
- **Calcium-to-phosphorus ratio:** dairy is high in calcium.
  - A diet near **2:1 Ca:P** improves calcium absorption and **lowers parathyroid hormone (PTH)**.
  - Chronic high PTH breaks down bone, and per Peat it lowers metabolic rate by reducing mitochondrial uncoupling.
  - High-phosphorus, low-calcium foods to limit: muscle meat, grains, soy, seeds, Brazil nuts, colas.
- **Associations cited:** better bone density, lower body fat and better body composition, lower blood pressure, less metabolic syndrome, and an inverse link with colon and breast cancer.
