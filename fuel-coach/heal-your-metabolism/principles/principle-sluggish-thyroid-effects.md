# Effects of a Sluggish Thyroid

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** weight gain, cholesterol, libido, liver, digestion, immunity, blood pressure, mood, sleep  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Statins, thyroid medication or blood-pressure concerns go to the client's provider.  
> **Related:** Signs of Healthy vs. Slow Metabolism · Sleep Protocol

**Weight gain**
- Low T3 means less energy and heat are produced, so more is stored.
- A **1 °C drop in body temperature cuts metabolic rate by 10–13%**. On a 2,000 kcal maintenance diet that is about **200–230 kcal/day less** burned.

**High cholesterol**
- T3 plus vitamin A convert cholesterol into the steroid hormones: pregnenolone, progesterone, DHEA, testosterone, estrogen, cortisol and aldosterone.
- With low T3, cholesterol backs up in the blood.
- The book advises **checking thyroid before starting statins**. Refer this to a provider.

**Sex hormones**
- Under stress, available thyroid and cholesterol are diverted to cortisol, at the cost of sex hormones.
- Effects: low libido, PMS, irregular cycles, infertility, dry skin and hair, muscle loss.
- The book's formula: "Reduced stress = increased thyroid = increased sex hormones."

**Liver**
- The liver runs on its own glycogen and needs thyroid hormone to use it. It also does much of the T4 → T3 conversion.
- A sluggish liver clears less estrogen, insulin, ammonia, drugs and toxins.

**Digestion and immunity**
- Low metabolism means low stomach acid (HCl). That causes poor protein, mineral and B12 absorption, bacterial overgrowth, bloating and IBS-type symptoms.
- About 70% of the immune system is in the gut.
- Cortisol breaks down the thymus, which makes T-cells, so immunity drops.

**Blood pressure**
- Chronic stress keeps the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system (RAAS) switched on, which raises blood pressure.

**Mood**
- An early "cortisol high" eventually gives way to fatigue and depression.

**Sleep**
- Poor liver glycogen storage means blood sugar can't be held overnight.
- Adrenaline and cortisol rise, causing night waking and insomnia (see "Sleep Protocol").
