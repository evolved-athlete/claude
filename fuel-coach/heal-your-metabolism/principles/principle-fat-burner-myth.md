# The "Fat Burner" Myth

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** fat adaptation, glucose oxidation, keto, fat burning at rest  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Carbohydrates: Sugar as the Preferred Fuel · Exercise and the Thyroid

- The body always uses a mix of glucose, fat and protein.
- The book's view: **during activity most energy should come from glucose oxidation**, because it gives the most heat and CO₂.
- Training the body to be a "fat burner" makes it more efficient (slower) and impairs glucose metabolism.
- **At rest, muscle and heart prefer fat.** So **sleep and more muscle mass** are the "safest" ways to burn fat.
