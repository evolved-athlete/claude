# Stress and the General Adaptation Syndrome

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** stress, Selye, adrenal fatigue  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Thyroid vs. Stress Hormones

1. **Alarm:** freeze, or fight-or-flight.
2. **Resistance:** fat and muscle breakdown and glucose mobilization. The body recovers if the stress ends.
3. **Exhaustion:** fatigue, depression, weight change, immune and digestive problems, disease.
   - What's called "adrenal fatigue" is, per the author, really **suppressed thyroid**.

The body adds all stressors together: diet, training, work, relationships. "A good diet will only go so far" with 80-hour weeks or an unhappy life.
