# Supplements: Food First

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** supplements, multivitamins, vitamin D3, real food  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Nutrient Food Sources · Eggshell Calcium Protocol

- The book is against most pills.
- High-dose multivitamins showed no mortality, CVD or cancer benefit in a study of more than 400,000 people.
- Supplements don't heal and can mask problems. **Use food as the supplement.**
- Exception mentioned: liquid vitamin D3 (Carlson's) if sun and dairy are inadequate.
