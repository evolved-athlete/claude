# Grains and Gluten

> **Type:** Principle  
> **Topics:** grains, gluten, celiac, hidden gluten, gluten-free products  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** 30-Day Grain Elimination Test

- **Avoid all grains while healing a damaged metabolism.** The book's reasons:
  - Starch is pure glucose. Wheat bread raises blood sugar faster than sugar.
  - Grains have a high phosphorus-to-calcium ratio.
  - They contain oxalates (buckwheat and whole wheat worst; white rice lowest), phytates, trypsin inhibitors and lectins.
  - They contain gluten and gluten-like prolamines. The book counts corn, rice and quinoa proteins as capable of similar reactions.
- **Celiac/gluten-sensitivity symptoms:**
  - fatigue, brain fog, depression
  - diarrhea or constipation, bloating, gas
  - weight loss or gain
  - skin issues
  - joint or back pain
  - thin bones
- **Hidden gluten:**
  - beer, soy sauce, ketchup, sauces, dressings
  - ice cream, some candy
  - lunch meat, bouillon cubes, seitan
  - seasoned rice and chips, fried foods
  - cosmetics
- **Processed "gluten-free" products are not health foods.** They are full of gums, soy and synthetic vitamins, and about 30% exceed gluten limits anyway.
