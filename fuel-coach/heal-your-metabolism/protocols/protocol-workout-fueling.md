# Workout Fueling

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** pre-workout, during workout, post-workout, chocolate milk  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Kate's Miracle Drink · Homemade Chocolate Milk

**Workout fueling**
- **Before** a 30–60 minute session: **200–400 kcal**, 30–60 minutes ahead. Never train fasted.
  - Examples: eggs + OJ, fruit + cheese, a milk smoothie.
- **During:** optional if fed beforehand. Sip **Kate's Miracle Drink** (see recipe): sugar, gelatin, salt and minerals.
  - In winter, warm broth works as a pre-, during- or post-workout drink.
- **After:** muscles are insulin-sensitive for **up to 2 hours**. Have carbs + protein.
  - If no meal is coming, use a milk drink: homemade chocolate milk, milk + honey + salt, or an orange creamsicle.
  - Or: broth + pinch of salt + coconut oil + 1 cup OJ.
