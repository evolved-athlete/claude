# Blood-Sugar Balancing Rules

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** meal size, meal frequency, snacks, eating slowly, alcohol, coffee, headaches  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Clients with diabetes or on blood-sugar medication should make changes with their provider.  
> **Related:** The Pairing Rule: Carb + Protein + Fat · Meal Templates

## Basics
- The normal range is about **65–105 mg/dL**.
- **Too low** comes from long gaps between meals, stress, over-exercise, dieting, caffeine or alcohol without food. The body responds with adrenaline, cortisol and glucagon, which break down tissue.
- **Too high** comes from too much food, the wrong macros or eating too fast. That causes an insulin spike: glycogen fills first, then the excess goes to fat.

## Rules
1. **Size meals right.**
   - Most meals are **300–700 kcal**; snacks are **100–300 kcal**.
   - A meal should hold hunger off for **3–4 hours**.
   - Afterward the person should feel alert, happy and warm, not stuffed, sleepy or wired.
2. **Set meal frequency by liver glycogen capacity.**
   - **Most people: at least 6 eating occasions** (3 meals + 3 snacks).
   - **Significant metabolic issues** (hypoglycemia, diabetes, sugar sensitivity): **8–10 small meals**.
   - Space meals further apart as the liver heals.
3. **Eat when demand is high** (working, training, thinking). **Eat less when it is low** (lounging, late evening).
4. **Eat slowly and mindfully.**
   - Take **20–30 minutes per meal** and chew each bite **10–15 times**.
   - No eating while driving or in front of a screen.
5. **Remove** processed food and recreational drugs while healing.
6. **Alcohol**
   - Alcohol on its own drops blood sugar. Avoid it while healing.
   - If drinking, do it with food and/or with **fruit juice**. Per the book, fructose clears alcohol from the liver about 80% faster.
7. **Coffee and tea**
   - Never drink them black on an empty stomach. Add milk or cream plus sugar.
   - Jitters, nausea or anxiety after coffee mean the drink needs more sugar and fat.
8. **No "nothing after 7 pm" rule.** Fasting 3–4 hours before bed depletes liver glycogen and causes night waking. Use a bedtime snack (see "Sleep Protocol").

## Headaches
- These often come from unbalanced blood sugar or too little good sugar and food.
- Fix: many small, balanced meals.
