# Sugar-Sensitive Person Protocol

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** sugar cravings, sugar sensitivity, adding carbs back  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Clients with diabetes should make changes with their provider.

- Cutting out all sugar is a short-term fix that worsens dysfunction long term. The body will make glucose from its own tissue anyway.
- Add the right carbs back **slowly**, always paired with fat and protein.
- Start with the easiest sugars: pulp-free OJ, honey, cooked fruit, milk.
