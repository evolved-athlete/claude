# Eggshell Calcium Protocol

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** calcium, eggshell, dairy-free, dose  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Clients with kidney stones, kidney disease or on calcium-affecting medication should check with their provider.

- **For whom:** people who don't eat enough dairy. Dairy is the preferred calcium source.
- An eggshell is about 90% calcium carbonate, plus trace minerals. One shell (~6 g) holds about 2,400 mg calcium.
- **Doses:**
  - ⅛ tsp ≈ 1 g calcium carbonate ≈ 400 mg calcium
  - ¼ tsp ≈ 600 mg calcium carbonate, as listed in the recipe
- RDA is about 1,300 mg/day; Ray Peat suggests about 2,400 mg.
- **On a low-dairy diet: ¼ tsp, 1–3×/day, with food or OJ.** Calcium alone can irritate an empty stomach.
- Absorption equals calcium citrate and is not dependent on stomach acid (study cited). It has lower heavy metals than oyster-shell calcium.
- See recipe "Eggshell Calcium".

## Mineral broth
- **1–2 shots daily**, for magnesium, vitamin K and B vitamins. See recipe "Eggshell Calcium".
