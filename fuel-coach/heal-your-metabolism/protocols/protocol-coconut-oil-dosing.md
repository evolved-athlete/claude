# Coconut Oil Dosing Protocol

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** coconut oil, MCT, loose stools  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Saturated Fats: The Preferred Fats

1. Start at **1 tsp/day**.
2. Add **1 tsp each week**.
3. Work up to **1–2 Tbsp/day**.

- Ways to use it: cooking, soups, broth, smoothies, dressing, or a spoonful on its own.
- Watch the response: warmer? better digestion? fewer illnesses?
- **If stools get too loose, cut back** until adapted.
- Replacing PUFA oils with coconut oil and butter can also start at about ½ Tbsp, adding a little each week. In a case example, temperature rose within 2 weeks.
