# Gut-Healing Carbohydrates

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** damaged gut, cooked fruit, low fiber  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** The Fiber Argument

- pulp-free OJ
- honey
- **cooked, skinless** apples, pears, peaches and nectarines
- watermelon
- papaya and other tropical fruit

With gut problems, **eat less fiber, not more.**
