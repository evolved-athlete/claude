# Raw Carrot Protocol

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** raw carrot, endotoxin, estrogen, gut  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

- Eat **1–2 raw carrots a day**, grated as a carrot salad (see recipe "Raw Carrot Salad") or whole.
- **Purpose, per the book:**
  - Its indigestible fiber binds and clears endotoxin and excess estrogen from the gut.
  - It supports gut healing.
  - It replaces a daily green salad.
- Used in cases of estrogen dominance, soy history and gut issues.
