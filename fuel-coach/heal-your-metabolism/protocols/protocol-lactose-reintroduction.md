# Lactose Reintroduction Protocol

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** lactose intolerance, adding milk back, gut healing  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Diagnosed milk allergy (not intolerance) is a medical matter; do not reintroduce without the provider.

The book attributes much intolerance to small-intestine damage from grains, PUFA, alcohol, drugs and additives, which lowers lactase.

1. **Heal the gut first** (Ray Peat says this can take as little as about 2 weeks).
   - Remove grains, nuts, raw vegetables and legumes.
   - Add bone broth, coconut oil, gelatin and a daily raw carrot.
2. Test **additive-free cheese** first (aged cheese has little lactose): Parmigiano-Reggiano, ricotta.
3. Start milk at **1–2 oz once a day, every day**.
4. **Add about 1 oz/day each week** until reaching the goal. In the case example, the client went up 2 oz/week to 3–4 cups/day.

A survey cited in the book: 81% of self-reported lactose-intolerant people had no symptoms on raw milk.
