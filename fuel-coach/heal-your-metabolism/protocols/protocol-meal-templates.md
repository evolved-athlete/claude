# Meal Templates

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** meal plan, daily structure, what to eat in a day  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Author's 5-Day Sample Week

| Time | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | Protein + fat + sugar + coffee with milk and sugar | 2 eggs in coconut oil + 1 cup cooked fruit + OJ with gelatin + coffee with milk and sugar |
| Snack | Sugar + protein + raw carrot | 8 oz OJ + 1 Tbsp gelatin + ½ cup cottage cheese + 1 raw carrot |
| Lunch | White fish or shellfish + root vegetable with butter + fruit (+ broth if meat) | 4 oz cod + ½ sweet potato with butter + 1 cup fruit |
| Snack | Milk + OJ + gelatin + salt | 8 oz milk + 8 oz OJ + 1 Tbsp gelatin + salt |
| Dinner | Protein + cooked vegetable with butter + fruit | 4 shrimp + 1 cup squash with butter + fruit + small carrot/tomato/cucumber salad |
| Bedtime | Milk + honey + salt | 8 oz milk + 1 Tbsp honey + salt |
