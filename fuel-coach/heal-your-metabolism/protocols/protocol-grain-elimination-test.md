# 30-Day Grain Elimination Test

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** elimination diet, gluten test, reintroduction  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Refer out:** Suspected celiac disease should be tested by a provider before eliminating gluten, because elimination can mask test results.

1. Remove **all** grains, grain products, gluten-free products and gluten-containing additives for **at least 30 days**. Reducing isn't enough.
2. Reintroduce and observe.
3. No change means the person likely tolerates grains. Even then, never 6–9 servings/day.

**Grains allowed once healthy (moderation, always with fat):** white or jasmine rice, slow-cooked oatmeal, sourdough, masa harina.
