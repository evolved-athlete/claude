# Author's 5-Day Sample Week

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** sample meal plan, calories, macros, high-nutrient day  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

| Day | Type | Carbs | Protein | Fat | kcal | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rest | 239 g | 113 g | 49 g | 1,870 | Eggs, cod, shrimp, OJ + gelatin snacks, milk + honey at bedtime |
| 2 | Workout | 280 g | 122 g | 59 g | 2,167 | Sweet potato–banana pancakes, Miracle Drink during, chocolate milk after, beef patty + **8 oz broth**, spaghetti squash with seafood marinara |
| 3 | High-nutrient | 268 g | 116 g | 52 g | 2,004 | **Liver and onions** breakfast, 16 oz broth lunch, **5 oz oysters** dinner |
| 4 | Workout | 270 g | 115 g | 60.5 g | 2,084 | Spaghetti squash quiche, raw carrot salad, Miracle Drink, chocolate milk, sole in butter, potato–butternut soup |
| 5 | Rest | 216 g | 118 g | 51 g | 1,795 | Breakfast smoothie, tuna + carrot, cod with coconut sauce, milk + honey at bedtime |

That works out to roughly **50–55% carbs, about 23% protein and about 22–25% fat**.
