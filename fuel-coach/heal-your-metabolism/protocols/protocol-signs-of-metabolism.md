# Signs of Healthy vs. Slow Metabolism

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** symptoms, intake questions, self-assessment, low metabolism signs  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".  
> **Related:** Temperature and Pulse Tests

## Signs of a healthy metabolism
These come from Barnes and the author:
- Warm body: **97.8–98.6 °F (36.6–37 °C)**
- Resting pulse **75–90 bpm** (Ray Peat)
- Energy all day; deep, restful sleep of **7–9 uninterrupted hours**
- Good digestion; **1–3 bowel movements a day**
- **Infrequent urination, about 4–6 times a day**
- Healthy skin, shiny hair, strong nails, bones and teeth
- Healthy libido and fertility; no PMS
- Clear mind; happy mood
- Lean and muscular without hours of exercise; rarely ill
- Blood pressure about 120/80

## Signs the metabolism is "on the fritz"
- Always cold, often **95–97 °F**, especially hands, feet and nose
- **Pulse under 75 bpm**
- Urinating all day; thirst never quenched
- Constipation, bloating, gas
- Poor sleep, waking tired, fatigue, depression, foggy thinking
- Low or absent sex drive
- Dry skin, hair and nails; slow wound healing; frequent illness
- Gains weight easily, loses it with difficulty; muscle loss with fat gain

Even a few of these may point to a depressed metabolism. The author argues that lifestyle, stress and diet matter more than age.

**The book's self-assessment questions** (useful as client intake questions):
1. What is your body temperature? Do you feel cold all the time: cold hands, feet, nose?
2. What is your resting pulse?
3. How often do you urinate per day? Do you have chronic thirst?
4. How often do you have a bowel movement? Constipation, bloating, gas, diarrhea?
5. What does your stool look like? Runny, pebbles, smelly, undigested food?
6. How is your sleep? Restless, interrupted, exhausted on waking?
7. How are your skin, hair and nails? Dry, brittle, slow-growing? Do you heal slowly?
8. How are your hormones? Low libido, PMS, infertility?
9. How is your energy? Dragging, highs and lows, mid-day crash, wired at night?
10. How is your mood? Anxious, depressed, irritable, sad?
11. Do you get sick often, or never?
12. Do you gain weight easily? Any edema (water retention)?
13. Is it hard to lose weight even with diet and exercise?
