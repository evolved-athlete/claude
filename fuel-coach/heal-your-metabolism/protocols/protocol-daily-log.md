# Daily Log

> **Type:** Protocol  
> **Topics:** food log, Cronometer, tracking, temperature log  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015). These are the author's positions (influenced by Ray Peat), not mainstream consensus. Present them as "the book recommends...".

- Record each day:
  - meal times, amounts and macros
  - temperature and pulse (waking and after meals)
  - how each meal makes you feel
  - sleep
  - bowel movements
- Suggested tool: **Cronometer**.
- Logging shows under- or over-eating, macro imbalances and foods that drop temperature or pulse or affect mood and sleep.
- "If you are not logging you will only be guessing."
