# Orange Creamsicle

> **Type:** Recipe  
> **Topics:** post-workout, milk, orange juice  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015), Chapter 20.

**C 25 / P 14 / F 5 g, 201 kcal**

- ½ cup pulp-free OJ (fresh-squeezed is best)
- 1 cup 2% milk
- 1 Tbsp gelatin
- pinch of salt
- optional: ½ cup Häagen-Dazs vanilla ice cream

Directions: blend or shake everything and sip after the workout. Add ice for a thicker, colder drink.
