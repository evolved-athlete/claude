# Breakfast Smoothie

> **Type:** Recipe  
> **Topics:** breakfast, smoothie, milk, egg  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015), Chapter 20.

**Serves 1; C 35 / P 21 / F 9 g, 315 kcal**

Ingredients:
- 1 cup 2% milk
- ¾ cup frozen fruit
- 1 raw pastured egg
- 1 Tbsp gelatin
- 1 Tbsp sugar or honey
- pinch of white sea salt

Directions: blend until smooth.
