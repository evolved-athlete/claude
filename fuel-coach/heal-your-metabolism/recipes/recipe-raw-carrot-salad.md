# Raw Carrot Salad

> **Type:** Recipe  
> **Topics:** snack, raw carrot  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015), Chapter 20.

**Serves 2; C 7 / P 0 / F 3 g, 55 kcal**

Ingredients:
- 2 raw medium carrots
- ½ Tbsp melted coconut oil
- ½ Tbsp white vinegar
- salt to taste

Directions:
1. Grate the carrots lengthwise.
2. Mix in the coconut oil and vinegar.
3. Salt to taste.

Eat with cottage cheese or Parmesan for protein and fat.
