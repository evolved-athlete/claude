# Milk and Honey

> **Type:** Recipe  
> **Topics:** post-workout, bedtime snack, sleep  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015), Chapter 20.

**C 32 / P 14 / F 5 g, 229 kcal**

- 1½ cups 2% milk
- 1 Tbsp gelatin
- 1 Tbsp raw honey
- pinch of salt
- optional: ½ cup vanilla ice cream

Milk and Honey also doubles as the bedtime snack.

Directions: blend or shake everything and sip after the workout. Add ice for a thicker, colder drink.
