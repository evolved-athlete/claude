# Homemade Chocolate Milk

> **Type:** Recipe  
> **Topics:** post-workout, milk, cacao  
> Source: Kate Deering, *How to Heal Your Metabolism* (2015), Chapter 20.

**C 32 / P 21 / F 12 g, 320 kcal**

- 1½ cups 2% organic milk
- 1 Tbsp raw cacao
- 1 Tbsp sugar or raw honey
- 1 Tbsp gelatin
- pinch of salt

Directions: blend or shake everything and sip after the workout. Add ice for a thicker, colder drink.
